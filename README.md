# 📚 Harper Collins 📚

### HarperCollins Publishers Workshop Performance Dashboard

An interactive Excel dashboard designed to analyze workshop performance across regions, cities, schools, and subjects helping management monitor workshop execution, resource allocation, school engagement, and revenue generation through dynamic business insights.

### Description 

The HarperCollins Publishers Workshop Performance Dashboard is an interactive Excel-based business intelligence solution developed to monitor and evaluate educational workshops conducted across different regions and schools. The dashboard enables stakeholders to analyze workshop trends, regional performance, subject demand, school participation, and RP (Resource Person) fee distribution, supporting data-driven operational and strategic decisions.

### Tech Stack

The dashboard was built using the following tools and technologies:<br>
• 📊 Microsoft Excel – Primary platform for building the interactive dashboard.<br>
• 📂 Power Query – Used for cleaning, transforming, and preparing raw workshop data.<br>
• 📈 Pivot Tables & Pivot Charts – Used for dynamic summarization and visualization.<br>
• 🎛 Slicers – Enabled interactive filtering by Month and Region.<br>
• 🧮 Excel Functions – Used for KPI calculations, aggregations, and business metrics.<br>
• 🔗 Data Modeling – Structured datasets to enable efficient analysis across schools, cities, regions, subjects, and workshops.<br>
• 📁 File Format – .xlsx for development and .png for dashboard presentation.

### Data Source

Source: HarperCollins Publishers Workshop Performance Dataset (Internal Business Data)

The dataset contains records of workshops conducted across multiple cities, schools, and regions. It includes information such as:<br>
• Workshop Date & Month<br>
• Region<br>
• City<br>
• School Name<br>
• Workshop Subject<br>
• Internal & External Resource Persons (RPs)<br>
• RP Fees<br>
• User & Non-User Schools<br>
• Workshop Counts<br>
The data was transformed using Power Query before being analyzed through Pivot Tables and interactive Excel visualizations.

### Features / Highlights

-> Business Problem

HarperCollins Publishers conducts hundreds of educational workshops across various regions and schools. Tracking workshop performance, understanding regional demand, monitoring school participation, and analyzing RP costs manually becomes increasingly difficult as the volume of data grows.

Business teams needed a centralized reporting solution to answer questions such as:<br>
• Which months recorded the highest number of workshops?<br>
• Which regions conduct the most workshops?<br>
• Which subjects are most in demand?<br>
• Which cities contribute the highest workshop volume?<br>
• How many schools are participating?<br>
• What is the ratio of User vs Non-User schools?<br>
• How are Internal and External Resource Persons distributed?<br>
• Which regions generate the highest RP Fee?

-> Goal of the Dashboard

The dashboard was developed to provide a single interactive platform that enables management to:<br>
• Monitor workshop performance across regions and months.<br>
• Evaluate school participation and engagement.<br>
• Analyze workshop demand by subject and city.<br>
• Compare Internal vs External Resource Person utilization.<br>
• Track RP Fee distribution across regions.<br>
• Support operational planning and resource allocation using real-time insights.

-> Walkthrough of Key Visuals

📌 KPI Cards

The dashboard highlights key business metrics at a glance:<br>
• Average Workshops per City<br>
• Average Workshops per School<br>
• Total RP Fee<br>
• Total Schools<br>
• Unique Schools<br>
These KPIs provide an overall snapshot of workshop performance and organizational reach.

📅 Month & Region Slicers<br>
Interactive slicers allow users to filter the entire dashboard by:<br>
• Month<br>
• Region<br>
This enables quick analysis of workshop performance across different business segments.

📊 Month-wise Workshops<br>
Displays the total workshops conducted each month.

This helps identify:<br>
• Seasonal trends<br>
• Peak operational months<br>
• Periods requiring additional planning or resource allocation

🌍 Region-wise Workshops<br>
A donut chart showing the percentage distribution of workshops across different regions.

It helps management identify:<br>
• High-performing regions<br>
• Underperforming territories<br>
• Regional workload distribution

📚 Subject-wise Workshops<br>
Shows the number of workshops conducted for each educational subject.

This visual helps identify:<br>
• Most popular workshop topics<br>
• Subject demand trends<br>
• Areas requiring additional educational content

🏫 User vs Non-User Schools<br>
A clustered column chart comparing User and Non-User schools across different months.

This allows stakeholders to evaluate:<br>
• Customer engagement<br>
• Adoption trends<br>
• Opportunities for expanding workshops into new schools

👥 Internal vs External RP<br>
A horizontal comparison chart displaying the proportion of Internal and External Resource Persons across regions.

This helps management optimize:<br>
• Resource utilization<br>
• Training requirements<br>
• Staffing strategies

📍 City-wise Workshops<br>
A line chart illustrating workshop distribution across cities.

It enables users to:<br>
• Identify high-performing cities<br>
• Detect geographic demand patterns<br>
• Support regional expansion planning

💰 RP Fee / Business Summary<br>
Compares RP Fee and overall business contribution by region.

This visualization assists in:<br>
• Budget monitoring<br>
• Cost analysis<br>
• Financial performance evaluation<br>
• Resource investment decisions

-> Business Impact & Insights

• Performance Monitoring: Provides a centralized view of workshop execution across multiple regions and schools.<br>
• Resource Optimization: Helps balance Internal and External Resource Person allocation based on regional demand.<br>
• Strategic Planning: Identifies high-performing cities, regions, and subjects to guide future workshop planning.<br>
• Customer Engagement: Tracks User vs Non-User school participation to uncover opportunities for expanding educational outreach.<br>
• Financial Visibility: Monitors RP Fee distribution and business contribution, enabling better budgeting and cost management.<br>
• Operational Efficiency: Reduces manual reporting efforts by consolidating workshop metrics into an interactive dashboard, allowing faster and more informed decision-making.
